Usage terms

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This is a personal backup project under development. Features, operating procedures and retention settings may change as they are implemented and verified.

Purpose

The utility is intended for the owner's authorized PostgreSQL backup and recovery work. This public website explains that purpose and its data practices. It does not provide a public backup service, customer registration, Google sign-in or access to backup archives.

Authorized use

Use the utility only with databases, accounts and information you own or are authorized to administer. The account owner controls Google authorization and the storage locations used for recovery copies.

Recovery responsibilities

A completed upload alone does not prove that a database can be recovered. Verify a restore before relying on a backup, retain an accessible recovery copy of encryption keys and review available storage before expanding retention. No uptime or recovery-time guarantee is offered by this development project.

Privacy and third-party services

The Privacy Policy explains intended Google-data access, storage, retention and deletion. Google Drive, the VPS provider and GitHub Pages operate under their own terms and policies. Do not place credentials, tokens, private keys or database archives in this public site's repository.
