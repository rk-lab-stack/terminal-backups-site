Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This personal backup utility is under development. Google Drive authorization and the destination account are verified. A manual encrypted backup has been uploaded, downloaded and restored successfully in an isolated test database. Scheduled backups and automated retention are not active.

Purpose and owner

The utility is intended to create and recover the owner's PostgreSQL backups using the owner's VPS and Google Drive. It is a personal project, with no public customer sign-up or active automated backup collection. Contact the owner at capitalvision4fx@gmail.com.

Google access and the data involved

The configured Google permission is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. When authorized, the integration is intended to manage backup files and folders it creates. It does not request Gmail permissions or read email.

The backup workflow may process database archives, recovery metadata, file names and identifiers, timestamps, sizes, checksums and verification results. Google account details and storage quota may be checked to confirm the destination account and available capacity. Archives can contain any information the owner stores in the source database.

OAuth client credentials and access or refresh tokens enable authorized Google access. They must be stored in private configuration, separately from public website files, application source published on GitHub and backup logs.

How information is used

Data is used for creating, storing, checking and restoring the owner's backups, managing retention, reporting failures and confirming available storage. It is not used for advertising, sold to data brokers or used to train AI models.

Use and transfer of Google API data is limited to the backup and recovery purposes described here and is intended to follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Material changes to Google-data access or use require updated disclosures and any necessary renewed consent.

Storage, encryption and service providers

The manual workflow creates archives on the VPS and encrypts their contents before transfer to Google Drive. Recovery checks download and decrypt a copy on the VPS, then compare its isolated restore with the source. The first complete check has passed. File and folder names remain readable. A private recovery copy of the encryption keys is held outside the VPS; the keys and backup archives are excluded from this public website.

Google Drive stores uploaded backup files, and the VPS processes source and restored database data. These providers handle information according to their services and privacy terms. GitHub Pages hosts these public information pages; it does not receive the database archives or OAuth tokens through this website.

Retention

The initial proposed policy keeps up to two verified regular local backups and one pre-change copy, with up to seven daily and four weekly copies on Google Drive, subject to a 10 GB cloud backup budget and available capacity. Automated retention is not currently active. Retention settings may be revised as the project develops, and any material change will be reflected in this policy.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke the utility's Google access through Google Account third-party connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically remove copies already created. When discontinuing use, the owner can also remove the utility's stored Google OAuth tokens from its private authorization configuration.

Delete unwanted archives from Google Drive and, where appropriate, empty its trash. VPS copies, downloaded copies and copies on other storage must be deleted separately by their owner. Before deleting recovery material or keys, consider whether another usable recovery copy remains.

This website

These static pages contain no account forms, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or external fonts. They do not read Google Drive or collect database files. GitHub Pages logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Questions and updates

For privacy, deletion or access questions, contact capitalvision4fx@gmail.com. Check the date above for the latest revision. This policy should be updated as the implemented data practices change.
